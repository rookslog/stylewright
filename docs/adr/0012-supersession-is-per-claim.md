# ADR-0012 — Supersession is per claim, by a person

Blanket supersession was a draft 1 claim, and it was wrong: a new study on
a new prompt, build, or delivery mode measures a different thing, so
nothing about being newer makes a figure a replacement.

**Decision.** A new figure supersedes an old one only when a person judges
the two comparable and links them, per claim, with an explicit link in
both directions.

**Consequences.** No figure is retired by the mere existence of a
successor, and no claim inherits authority from a study that measured
something else. What happens to unaudited figures that nothing supersedes
is a separate decision, and ADR-0014 records it — this ADR carried both
until a review caught the pair breaking the one-decision rule.

Decided 2026-08-05. The mechanism is the measurement design, section 3.
